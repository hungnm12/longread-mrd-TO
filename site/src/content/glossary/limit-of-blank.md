---
id: GLOSSARY-008
term: Limit of blank / limit of detection
short_definition: Analytical-validation endpoints describing what a blank sample produces and the lowest reliably detected level.
---

**Limit of blank (LoB)** is the highest score a sample containing no analyte is expected to produce.
**Limit of detection (LoD)** is the lowest true level that is reliably distinguished from that blank.

Estimating either requires replicates: many blank measurements to characterise the blank
distribution, and many measurements at each level to establish reliability.

**This project cannot estimate them.** It has one replicate per dilution level and a single 0%
control. The dilution series supports *relative comparison between models under matched conditions*,
and the 0% control serves as a specificity anchor — nothing more.
