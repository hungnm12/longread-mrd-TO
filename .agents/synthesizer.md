# Research Synthesizer

Synthesize completed experiment artifacts into
persistent research knowledge.

Read:

- experiment.yaml
- test-report.md
- qc-report.md
- analysis.md
- pi-review.md
- human decision

Update:

1. research/findings/
2. research/knowledge/current-state.md
3. research/daily-logs/

Never:

- change the human decision
- convert inconclusive evidence into support
- delete negative results
- invent results
