Tumor-only long-read MRD — daily report Rendered 2026-08-24

Daily report — 2026-08-24

Subject: why a day of literature survey ended up testing a within-sample statistic, and why that statistic was falsified the same day.

A note on naming. This report deliberately gives the tested statistic no proper name. It is not a new method belonging to this project — it is one instance of a published family (see §2). It is referred to by description: allele fraction computed within a single haplotype. Giving it a name of our own would be claiming authorship of something that is not ours.

One-line conclusion. The survey’s primary direction was falsified by the failure criterion it registered before the run — but the gap it was invented to close is still there, still measured, and does not depend on that direction having been right.

1. The question the day opened with: what is binding?

The day did not begin with “which idea should we try”. It began with which constraint blocks everything else — the binding constraint.

The answer came from a finding that already existed:

FIND-0002 — 30.4% of clustered PASS candidates carry their ALT allele on ≥2 reads of the matched normal, meaning inherited variation rather than tumor variation. 65.9% of those are absent from the SEQC2 somatic truth set.

Why this is binding rather than merely a bad number: every other research direction in this project sits downstream of it. Methylation, same-molecule linkage, MRD detection — all operate on the candidate set. If ~30% of that set is inherited variation, every statistic downstream is measuring partly noise. There is no way around it.

Consequence for direction-picking: candidate-set composition was ranked as the problem to solve first.

2. Literature review — what the field says, and where the gap is

2.1 The field says this is already solved

The systematic search of 2026-08-21 and the 80-source deep research run today converge on the same conclusion:

Source Contribution
SGZ (Sun et al., PLoS Comput Biol 2018) Somatic/germline separation without a matched normal, using allele fraction plus purity/ploidy/copy number
smrest (Simpson 2024, PMC10925087) Haplotype phasing as the primary information source for tumor-only somatic calling on long reads
ClairS-TO (Nat Commun 2025) Ships MultiHap, NoAncestry, and Verdict — Verdict described in the paper as “similar to and improved from SGZ”
UNMASC (PMC8494212) An unmatched-normal design

The lab’s conclusion on 2026-08-21, verbatim: “not a research question — it is an integration task”.

That conclusion is correct about the literature. Stopping there closes the direction.

2.3 The gap, stated precisely

Both of these are true and they do not conflict:

  • The field has a mechanism for matched-normal-free germline separation.
  • The shipped implementation, run on this ONT tumor-only data, removes approximately nothing.
One end The other end
Real germline contamination, measured with a matched normal: 30.4% (clustered candidates) · 28.0% (per candidate, chr1) Removed by the shipped filters: 0 PASS candidates across 5 runs

The distance between those two numbers is the gap. It is not the novelty of an idea — it is the novelty of a measurement. It is the only thing produced today that could open a paper.

4. The reasoning chain that led to the tested statistic

Five links, each anchored to a number above.

Link 1 — Identify the binding constraint. Candidate-set composition, ~30% private germline contamination (§1). Every other direction sits behind it.

Link 2 — The literature says it is solved. SGZ / smrest / MultiHap / NoAncestry / Verdict (§2.1). Normally the direction closes here.

Link 3 — The measurement contradicts the literature. 0 PASS candidates touched across 5 independent samples (§2.2). The gap is quantified at both ends (§2.3). This is the decisive link — it turns an “integration task” back into a measurable question.

Link 4 — The material turns out to be free. A haplotype-based mechanism needs reads assigned to haplotypes, which costs compute. E1 shows 261 GB already on disk since week 4. The cost of testing drops to near zero, and this is why the direction outranked the alternatives — equally worth doing, far cheaper, and killable in a single run.

Link 5 — The mechanism, in one sentence. An inherited variant defines one of the two ancestral haplotypes, so nearly every read of that haplotype carries it → the within-haplotype fraction ≈ 1. A subclonal somatic variant merely rides a haplotype in a fraction of cells → that fraction < 1. Whole-locus VAF cannot see the difference because it averages across both haplotypes.

This is where the argument broke, and it broke as it was being written, not when it was run. Link 5 depends on the word “subclonal”. The reason given for believing it: FIND-0002 measured HCC1395’s somatic pairs as largely subclonal — median ALT-ALT fraction 0.200, 45.7% below 0.15. Read again: 0.200 is a pair-level molecule statistic, not a per-candidate one. Measured per candidate today, somatic VAF is centred between 0.3 and 0.7. Not the distribution the argument required.

5. The falsification experiment and its result

EXP-S1-001 — designed to be killable in one run, on files that already exist, in hours rather than days. Every threshold was fixed before the run (08 §10–§11).

Leakage guard, and how it was enforced:

Rule What happened
The feature function must not see truth Its arguments are only the tumor BAM, the candidate VCF and the reference
Features must be written to disk before any label is read Feature step began 09:20:45Z and closed its file at 09:24; labelling began 09:26:28Z
The matched normal may be opened only at labelling It was
No threshold tuning after seeing labels AUC is threshold-free; the single reported operating point was fixed in advance from the mechanism

Result:
Candidates with features 3,925 (chr1)
Meeting the inclusion rule (inside a SEQC2 high-confidence region, labelled, ≥10 reads on both haplotypes) 1,627 — 556 germline, 1,071 somatic
AUC(whole-locus VAF) — baseline 0.6318 [0.6023–0.6595]
AUC(within-haplotype fraction) — condition 0.5972 [0.5716–0.6214]
ΔAUC −0.0346, paired bootstrap 95% CI [−0.0676, +0.0015]
Registered H0 (ΔAUC ≤ 0.02) not rejected

Pre-fixed operating point (≥ 0.90 → call germline): removes 520/556 germline (93.5%) but discards 772/1,071 somatic (72.1%); precision for germline is 0.4025. Not usable.

Distribution — the mechanism made visible: 468/556 germline (84.2%) sit at ≥ 0.95, exactly as predicted. But 688/1,071 somatic (64.2%) sit there too. Under whole-locus VAF the two classes peak in different places: germline at 0.60–0.75, somatic at 0.30–0.45.

Control 1 — haplotype-swap null. Reallocating each candidate’s tagged ALT reads across its two haplotypes at random (hypergeometric), depths held fixed: ΔAUC mean −0.0106, 95% range [−0.0177, −0.0033]. The observed −0.0346 falls below that range. Conditioning on the true haplotype assignment is worse than allocating the same ALT counts at random. The statistic is not merely uninformative on this material — it is anti-informative.

Control 2 — coverage stratification. ΔAUC = −0.0078 below the median tagged depth of 75, and −0.0604 above it. The deficit grows with depth rather than shrinking — the signature of a ceiling effect, not of noise.

Two reasons it died:

  1. Purity. HCC1395 is a ~100% pure tumor line. A clonal somatic variant occupies one haplotype exactly as an inherited heterozygote does. The statistic separates “occupies the whole haplotype” from “occupies part of it” — it does not separate “inherited” from “acquired early and clonally expanded”.
  2. The statistic’s own definition excludes the regions that need solving. 1,895 of 3,522 labelled in-region candidates never reach the comparison for want of reads on a second haplotype, and 809 (42.7%) have zero reads on one haplotype. Those are the hemizygous and loss-of-heterozygosity regions — where germline contamination is worst. The method is defined where the problem is mild and undefined where it is severe. More chromosomes do not fix this.

The failure criterion, written before the run (08 §11): “If ΔAUC ≤ 0.02, this direction is abandoned. No re-analysis with a different statistic may be substituted after the fact.” → The direction is abandoned. The publication plan stops at stage S1; S2–S5 do not run.

What FIND-0004 deliberately does NOT claim:

  • Not that haplotype information is useless for tumor-only calling in general. Only that this statistic, on a ~100%-purity sample, cannot work — because the two classes become structurally identical under it.
  • Not that the 28.0% germline contamination is irremovable by some other within-sample statistic. Only that this one does not remove it.
  • The setting where the premise would differ is a low-purity sample — and that is precisely the setting this material cannot test, because at 0.01% tumor fraction there is no tumor read population at a locus to assign a haplotype to.

9. What remains after the primary direction died

Three things, none of which depend on the falsified statistic:
The shipped matched-normal-free germline filters remove 0 PASS candidates; 0.002–0.011% of records across 5 independent ONT tumor-only runs
Private germline surviving into the tumor-only PASS set, chr1, per candidate 28.0% (1,098 / 3,925)
Best within-sample discrimination found so far whole-locus VAF at AUC 0.63 — and haplotype conditioning makes it worse

The third number deserves separate attention: the baseline is stronger than assumed. Any future proposal has to beat 0.63, not 0.5.

The decision belongs to a person, not to a tool

PUBLICATION-PLAN §8 proposes a reframing — from “we propose a method” to “we benchmark the problem and show that the shipped solutions do not work on this platform”:

How much germline survives tumor-only somatic calling on ONT long reads, and which matched-normal-free signal removes any of it? — measured across the six cell lines on this server against public truth, over ClairS-TO (H, MultiHap, NoAncestry, and Verdict once built) and DeepSomatic tumor-only, whose outputs already exist.

That is a benchmark contribution rather than a method contribution. PROJECT.md reserves a change of research direction to the researcher. Nothing has been started on it.

Honest position on venue: benchmarks do reach Genome Biology and Briefings in Bioinformatics, but usually with more breadth than six cell lines and one platform. Do not promise Q1.
