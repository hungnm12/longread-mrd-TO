Bounds on this project

"Dilute to 0.001% and still get a call" — the paper already ran that experiment, and it failed

A unit check settled it before any argument: 0.001% = 1×10⁻⁵, while SIMMA's LOD is 100 ppm = 1×10⁻⁴ = 0.01%. The ask was ten times below the reported limit.

Ceiling 1 — counting statistics
At 10⁻⁴ roughly 42 independent features suffice to hold the z-score. At 10⁻⁵ you would need about 4,200 — and only tens of independent copy-number segments exist. The features run out before the arithmetic does.
Ceiling 2 — the biological floor
Healthy-person variation in copy number, methylation and fragment length is 3–4 orders of magnitude larger than a 10⁻⁵ tumour signal. These are distribution-shift signals, so they sit in an already-salty pool.
Takeaway: to reach 10⁻⁵ you must change evidence type — to SNV (personalised, tumour-informed) or an SV/ecDNA junction, where the background is zero. At exactly 10⁻⁴ all you get is a scalar tumour fraction with an interval; SCNA, ecDNA and SNV are each already dead at that level.
