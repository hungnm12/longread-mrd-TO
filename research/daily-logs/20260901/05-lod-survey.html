Only two of six signal axes carry a limit of detection — and the paper never ran a single-modality ablation

A grep of the source for unimodal, single modality, alone, each data type returned nothing. The 10⁻⁴ figure belongs to the three signals combined, and no one knows which axis contributes what.

Takeaway: the two numbers that exist use two different definitions, and both are edges, not safe points — ecDNA's is a coin flip (50% detection), and the multimodal one is the point the model admits it is unsure (the changepoint where intervals start to widen). The missing ablation is exactly where this thesis can contribute.
