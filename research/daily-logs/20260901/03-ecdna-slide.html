And the overclaim the script caught, at 17:26
Writing the ~90-second speaking script exposed a label problem: the left panel says GENOME-WIDE SIGNAL, but the evidence behind the slide is ecDNA beating broad copy number only. SIMMA's reported multimodal LOD (~1×10⁻⁴) is in fact better than its reported ecDNA LOD (~1×10⁻³) — so as labelled, the slide invites the claim that ecDNA beats the whole model. A relabel to BROAD COPY NUMBER was proposed at 17:26 and again at 21:41. It is still not applied.
Takeaway: the editing rule that emerged today is one slide, one question, answered by the picture — and writing the script, not reviewing the slide, is what caught the claim the picture was quietly making.
