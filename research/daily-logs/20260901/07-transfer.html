The design trap named while checking the dilution question
Diluting every point into one shared control pool erases inter-individual variance and produces an artificially optimistic LOD. The fix is a paired dilution series — the same control at every point, versus a different control at every point — which measures the false-optimism gap directly. Not drafted.
