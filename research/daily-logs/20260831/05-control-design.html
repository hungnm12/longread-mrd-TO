Protocol governance

One confound, killed twice: is methylation selecting tumour reads, or is the filter just more aggressive?

Two of the fourteen findings were structural — without them the benchmark could have produced a clean win that meant nothing.

FindingWhat was wrongWhat the protocol now does instead
B001
review_001		 The control was arithmetically identical to the treatment. The outcome-matched random control was matched on B1's own answer, so it could never differ from it Control removed. Replaced by two controls matched on the cutoff rule, not on the answer: C1 permuted-score (read–score correspondence destroyed) and C2 CpG-density. Neither can equal B1 by construction
B014
review_004		 The confound came back through C1. C1 retains ~5% of ALT reads in every sample, while B1's measured retention on HCC1395 was 26% in the blank, 67% at 1%, 37–50% at 0.1%. A z-win over C1 could be counting statistics, not selection New preregistered condition S4 (differential retention): B1's ALT retention at 0.1% must exceed its retention in the blank, on both individuals, with C1's 5th/95th percentiles over 200 draws as the noise band
B003 The variance model was being chosen silently — two different constructions existed in the project record Both preregistered together: V1 plug-in binomial sd (frozen mrdz construction) and V2 Poisson √(mean_blank). S1 must hold under both
B006 S3 would have been decided by noise; two of five blank replicates went unused All five TF0 replicates executed per individual (24 → 28 BAM passes); S3 is leave-one-out over all five, failing if any of the ten blanks returns detected
— A pre-registration that its own results can revise is not one Named non-success outcomes that may not be relabelled: MECHANISM UNSUPPORTED, SD-MODEL DEPENDENT, NOT REPLICATED, FAILURE TO DEMONSTRATE. No p-value is computed, so none can be selected after the fact
Takeaway: a z-win over C1 alone does not establish the mechanism — S4 is the leg that carries the claim. The protocol says so in writing, before the first number exists.
