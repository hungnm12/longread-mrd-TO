QC-04 — COLO829's statistic runs on single-digit counts
B1 blank totals are 2 / 0 / 4 / 3 / 6 reads; 0.1% totals are 9 / 19 / 18. The headline S4 ratio of 2.9127 is computed from roughly three kept reads in the blank. Four significant figures overstate what three reads can support. One blank gives G = 0 → sample_sd = 0 → no estimate — the case §15 defined a priori, so handled rather than broken.
QC-05 — S3 holds, but thinly under V2
Two COLO829 blanks return UNRELIABLE under V2: rep3 at [−1.64, +3.15], rep5 at [+0.69, +4.31]. Neither is a false detection — both straddle the call threshold, which is what UNRELIABLE is for — and both sit in the same single-digit-count regime.
Takeaway: the redesign is now written with the result known, so QC bound it in advance — every new criterion must be strictly harder to satisfy, and the new criteria must be labelled POST HOC permanently, with the v1.0 preregistered outcome reported beside them in full.
