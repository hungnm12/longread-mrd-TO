The seven problems are not equal — P5 carries the paper

Why P5 is the hinge, in the project's own words
"P5 is the hinge — if low-input single-molecule cfDNA does not work, nothing else in the paper exists." Everything downstream (per-sample confidence, paediatric relapse, CSF for brain tumours) is conditional on it.
What P1–P2 are not
They open every liquid-biopsy paper, so quoting them explains nothing about SIMMA. A note that stops at P1–P4 answers "what are the problems" but never "what did this paper contribute".
Takeaway: SIMMA fills P5(b) with Random Forest + conformal prediction over concatenated covariate blocks — that is integration at sample level. The gap left is integration at molecule level, which is exactly where this thesis sits.
