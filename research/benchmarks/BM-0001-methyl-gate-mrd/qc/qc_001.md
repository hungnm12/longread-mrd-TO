## Verdict

The execution is clean. The **methodology is not sufficient for the claim its own registered
outcome invites**, and QC found this in the output rather than in the protocol.

`results/registered_outcome.txt` reads `H1 SUPPORTED`. That statement is a correct mechanical
application of the locked criteria. QC does not overturn it and it is preserved verbatim. QC's
finding is that criterion **S4 — the criterion installed specifically to carry the mechanism
claim — is passed by the confound it was written to exclude.**

### QC-07 — leakage, verified rather than asserted
Every path opened by every one of the 42 workers was captured to
`execution/files_opened.txt`: 31 paths, comprising the 28 dilution BAMs, the two candidate
frames and `mrdz/score.py`. **No matched-normal BAM, no SEQC2 or orthogonal truth VCF, no panel
of normals and no population database was opened.** The methylation consensus, confident-position
set and cutoff are fitted per sample on that sample alone, so nothing is pooled across samples
and no test information reaches any other sample. The candidate frames predate this benchmark
and carry recorded draw seeds. Leakage guards §14 are satisfied by evidence, not by declaration.

### QC-08 — reproducibility
`run_sample.py` reproduces `EXP-S1-022` step 5 on HCC1395 TF0 rep1 exactly on all seven
recorded quantities (consensus 1,153,835; confident 353,719; cut 0.0769; gated 39; kept 10;
retention 26%; unscorable 2,217). The primary metric independently reproduces the recorded
`EXP-S1-022` step 8 D4 intervals: B0 `zlo_min` at 0.1% is **+0.7396** against the recorded
**+0.74**, and B1's is **+2.2995** against the recorded **+2.30**. Two independent
implementations, written months apart, agree to four figures. The interval machinery was
imported from the checksummed `mrdz.score`, not reimplemented.

### QC-09 — negative results preserved

## Findings that route the workflow

### QC-01 — S4 is passed by the confound it was written to exclude
Category: **methodology ambiguity / criterion incapable of supporting the claim**
Routing: benchmark-designer

Review_004's B014 identified that a `z` win over the controls could be filter aggressiveness
rather than methylation selecting the right reads. S4 was added as the fix: B1's ALT retention
must be *differential*, higher at 0.1% than in the blank. Measured:

| individual | B1 retention ratio | C2 retention ratio | difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| HCC1395 | **1.6741** | **1.6839** | −0.0098 |
| COLO829 | **2.9127** | **2.9041** | +0.0086 |

C2 is the CpG-density control. It scores reads by **how many confident CpG positions they
carry** and uses no methylation *pattern* at all. It reproduces B1's differential-retention
signature to within 0.6% on one individual and 0.3% on the other, on both.

S4 asks only whether B1's ratio exceeds its own blank. It does. It does not ask whether B1's
ratio exceeds C2's — and it does not, on either individual. So the criterion installed to
establish that *methylation* drives the differential is satisfied identically by a filter that
knows nothing about methylation state. **S4 as locked cannot distinguish the mechanism it was
written to protect.**

This is not a claim that H1 is false. It is a claim that this benchmark, as locked, does not
establish the mechanism half of its own research question. §21 F3 rejects the density
explanation only on `zlo_min`, and QC-02 explains why that leg does not carry the weight.

### QC-02 — S1's control legs are evaluated at incomparable count scales
Category: **invalid comparison**
Routing: benchmark-designer

Gated totals at 0.1%:

| arm | HCC1395 `G` | COLO829 `G` | blank scorable-ALT retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | 22 / 25 / 28 | 9 / 19 / 18 | 0.363 / 0.092 |
| C1 | 1–3 | 2–3 | 0.046 / 0.041 |
| C2 | 2–3 | 2–7 | 0.038 / 0.021 |

B1 counts roughly **8 to 10 times as many reads** as either control. `z` improves with counting
statistics independently of selection quality, so `zlo_min(B1) > zlo_min(C1)` and
`> zlo_min(C2)` are being decided at different points on a precision curve, not at matched
aggressiveness. The controls match B1 on the *nominal* cutoff rule — 95th percentile over all
scorable reads, `K_B1` 4.91% and `k_C2` 5.00%, verified in `execution/precheck_validation.md` —
but B1's ALT reads sit far higher in its own score distribution than C2's do in its, so the
*realised* ALT retention diverges by an order of magnitude. Matching the rule did not match the
aggressiveness. B014's confound survived the fix intended for it.

The comparison that would settle it is not outcome-matched (that is the degenerate control
review_001's B001 removed). It is a **retention sweep**: trace each arm's `zlo_min` as a
function of realised ALT retention by varying the percentile, and ask whether B1's curve lies
above C2's *at the same retention*. That is computable from the same BAM passes and requires no
new methodological assumption.

### QC-03 — the registered outcome is about `zlo_min`, and the detection outcome is much narrower
Category: **claim control**
Routing: recorded; constrains the evidence summary

`zlo_min` improved for B1 over B0 in all four individual × construction cells. The **detection**
outcome under the frozen replicate rule changed in exactly **one of the four**:

| | V1 | V2 |
|---|---|---|
| HCC1395 B0 | 1% | 1% |
| HCC1395 **B1** | 1% | **1% and 0.1%** |
| COLO829 B0 | 1% | 1% |
| COLO829 B1 | 1% | 1% |

So B1 turns a non-detection into a detection at 0.1% **on one individual, under one of the two
preregistered variance constructions**. Under V1 on the same individual it does not, and on
COLO829 neither construction does. Any sentence of the form "methylation enables 0.1%
detection" is a sentence about one cell of that table. The evidence summary must say so.

### QC-04 — COLO829's B1 statistic runs on single-digit counts
Category: **statistical validity**
Routing: recorded; constrains interpretation

COLO829 B1 blank totals are **2 / 0 / 4 / 3 / 6** reads and its 0.1% totals are 9 / 19 / 18.
One blank replicate yields `G = 0`, hence `sample_sd = 0`, hence the `no estimate` verdict —
the case protocol §15 defines a priori, so this is handled rather than broken. But the S4
retention ratio of **2.9127** for COLO829 is computed from roughly three kept reads in the
blank. Four significant figures overstate what three reads can support. HCC1395's B1 blank
totals (10 / 8 / 8 / 8 / 6) are firmer but still small.

### QC-05 — S3 holds, with a thin margin under V2
Category: **near-miss, recorded**

No blank returns `detected` in any arm under either construction, so S3 holds as written. Two
COLO829 blanks come back `UNRELIABLE` under V2 — rep3 at [−1.64, +3.15] and rep5 at
[+0.69, +4.31]. Neither is a false detection. Both straddle the call threshold, which is what
`UNRELIABLE` is for, and both sit in the same single-digit-count regime as QC-04.

### QC-06 — executor deviations
Category: **none blocking**

D1 (BAM content hashes replaced by path + size + header + `@PG` chain, 2.3 TB of hashing
avoided), D2 (an implementation fault that raised an exception before producing any number),
D3 (B0's literal §16 retention exceeds 1 because its numerator includes unscorable reads;
the literal value is retained and a `retention_scorable_only` column added), D4 (the C1
retention band §21 F8 requires but §22's schema omitted), D5 (the exploratory arm's predicted
degeneracy). All five carry `Methodology changed: NO` and QC concurs with that classification
in every case. D3 and D4 are corrections of internal inconsistencies in the locked protocol and
should be folded into the next version rather than left as standing deviations.

F8 is evaluable given D4 and does not fire: C1's retention ratio is 0.857 with a 5–95 band of
[0.00, 1.96] on HCC1395 and 1.161 with [0.32, 2.35] on COLO829. Both bands contain 1, as the
permutation control's construction requires. **The control behaves as designed.**

## Constraint on the redesign, which QC states because it is the risk here

The observed result is now known, so the next protocol version is no longer written blind. Two
rules bind it:

1. **Every new criterion must be strictly harder to satisfy than what it replaces.** Requiring
   B1's differential retention to exceed C2's, and requiring a retention-matched comparison,
   can only make passing harder. Neither can be accused of being chosen to make the observed
   result positive.
2. **The new criteria are POST HOC and must be labelled so, permanently.** They were written
   after C2's retention ratio was seen. They may qualify or withdraw the v1.0 conclusion; they
   may not be reported as preregistered, and the v1.0 preregistered outcome must be reported
   beside them in full.

Forbidden here, explicitly: redefining the primary level, the call threshold, the gate, the
variance constructions, or the success criteria S1–S3 in any direction that makes the observed
`H1 SUPPORTED` easier to keep.
