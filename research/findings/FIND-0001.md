## What was asked

G1 rested on a proximity statistic: 34.8% of PASS candidates have another PASS candidate within
10 kb. Proximity is an opportunity, not an observation. The question was whether two candidates
are actually seen as ALT on the same molecule.

## What was done

Every candidate pair within 20 kb on the same contig was enumerated (29,074 pairs), 1,000 were
sampled with a fixed seed, and every read spanning both positions in the pure tumor BAM was
classified by what it carried at each position. SEQC2 membership was attached afterwards and used
only to stratify the report.

## The numbers

| | |
|---|---|
| Pairs sampled / evaluable | 1,000 / 999 |
| Pairs with ≥1 ALT-ALT read | **678 (67.9%)** — threshold for success was 5% |
| Pairs with ≥2 ALT-ALT reads | **662** — threshold was 100 |
| Reads covering both positions | 50,756 |
| ALT-ALT / one-ALT / REF-REF | 23.9% / 35.2% / 40.2% |
| Median per-pair ALT-ALT fraction | 0.41 (IQR 0.22–0.74) |

By distance: 78% of pairs under 1 kb, 73% at 1–5 kb, 67% at 5–10 kb, 63% at 10–20 kb — the decline
tracks the fall in reads covering both (median 72 → 28), which is what a read-length limit looks
like.

By SEQC2 stratum: both-somatic 65.1%, one-somatic 54.7%, neither 78.6%.

## Verdict against the pre-registered criteria

| Criterion | Result |
|---|---|
| ≥5% of pairs with an ALT-ALT read | **passed** — 67.9% |
| ≥100 pairs with ≥2 ALT-ALT reads | **passed** — 662 |
| ALT-ALT more common among somatic pairs | **failed** — it is less common (65.1% vs 78.6%) |

Two of three criteria pass. The one that failed is the one that would have shown the signal is
*somatic*, and it failed in the direction that points at composition: the candidate set's unknown
germline content is the most likely explanation for the excess in the "neither" stratum.

## What this changes

G1's mechanism is observable, so the gap survives as a candidate on physical grounds. But the
result promotes G5 — candidate composition — from a foundational nicety to the precondition it
already claimed to be: until the somatic, germline and artifact fractions of the candidate set
are known, a co-occurrence count cannot be attributed to tumor origin.

The next measurement is the blank comparison: the same statistic on the TF0 replicates and the
dilutions. If ALT-ALT co-occurrence at 0.1% and 0.01% is indistinguishable from the blanks,
linkage carries no usable signal where it matters, whatever it does at 100% tumor fraction.
