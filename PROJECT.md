# Project

## Research objective
Develop and evaluate long-read MRD methods using ONT-derived signals.

## Current problem
Determine whether long-read information such as somatic haplotyping
and methylation can improve tumor-derived molecule recognition.

## Current resources
- ONT sequencing data
- somatic SNVs
- phasing capability
- methylation calls
- simulated/dilution datasets
- relevant MRD papers

## Research principles
- Hypothesis before implementation
- Baseline before proposed method
- No data leakage
- Negative results must be retained
- All claims require evidence
- Human approves research direction

## Human authority
Only the human researcher may:
- approve an idea
- change the hypothesis
- approve a new experiment
- accept/reject scientific conclusions
